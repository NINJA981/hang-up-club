# Design System: Hang Up Club

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Practice Line"**

Hang Up Club is a quiet daylight notebook (stone paper, moss ink, generous margins) that holds exactly **one dark object: the phone line**. Everything you do around a call (the daily card, the streak, the replay, the family setup) lives on the notebook. The call itself lives on **The Line**: a near-black screen with AAA type and two big hardware-like circles. It looks the same in light and dark themes, because a phone call should look like a phone call.

The switch from notebook to line is the main visual event of every day. When you cut a scammer off, the Line breaks apart and falls away to show the notebook underneath, with the evidence written on it: "Hung up at 0:06. The scammer needed 0:40."

The system is built for older eyes and hands first:

- 20px body text, 56px minimum targets and 64px primary buttons
- Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for low-vision readers
- one accent, used like a signal

The tone is adult and warm. The player is a capable person practising a skill, so there are no mascots, badges, points-as-decoration, cartoon illustrations or exclamation-mark confetti copy. The only celebration is a real family member's voice.

**Blend notes (bible §10.2, archetype A2 "Calm consumer daily-use"):**

- **From oura** we took:
  - the idea of a warm resting "paper" as the default surface
  - one action colour per view, maximum
  - earth tones only in data (the moss tints of the streak calendar), never on buttons
  - long vertical silences between groups

  We rejected Oura's cream canvas (bible ban #15), its thin serif display (T7 is our assigned pairing), its pill CTAs and its photography-led layouts. Our version differs: the paper is **cool stone at C 0.004, hue 110**, not cream, and the only "image" is the product's own call screen.
- **From apple** we took:
  - **the colour change is the divider**: notebook (light) and Line (dark) are two worlds, with no borders needed between them
  - `scale(.95)` as the press state on the call circles
  - a single interactive accent
  - a comfortable reading body size, raised from Apple's 17px to **20px** for our audience

  We rejected Apple's blue, its frosted sub-nav and its product drop shadow.
- **From superhuman** we took:
  - warm-grey ink instead of black (`text` is a moss-tinted near-black, never `#000`)
  - **one CTA per band**
  - the closing dark band on the landing page, re-expressed as the Line

  We rejected the indigo and violet palette, the portrait hero and the pill hero CTA.

No brand names, colours, fonts or logos from these references appear anywhere in UI, copy, assets or code identifiers.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two worlds: the **Notebook** (theme-aware, light by default) and **The Line** (always dark, AAA).
- One accent (moss) for every positive interactive signal. Red exists only for Hang Up and the scammer's ASK marker.
- Senior-first scale: body 20/30, nothing below 18px, targets ≥ 56px, primary 64px, call circles 96px.
- Soft rectangles for actions (14px) and true circles for call hardware. No pills.
- Hairline-first depth on the notebook; tonal steps on the Line; shadows only on things that float.
- Motion is near-zero in daily use. One orchestrated moment, "The Cut", when a scam call is hung up.

### Named Rules
**The One Moss Rule.** At most one filled moss button per view, and moss covers ≤ 5% of the pixels on any notebook screen. The streak calendar's moss wash is data, not chrome.

**The Red Means Them Rule.** Red marks only two things: the Hang Up circle (your exit) and the ASK marker (their trap). Errors use text plus an icon, in `danger` only when something is destructive. Red is never decoration, never used for a badge, and never used for "wrong" on a legitimate-call miss (misses use neutral text and an info icon).

**The Line Is Always Dark Rule.** The call screen uses `call-*` tokens in both themes. A light-theme incoming call is still a dark screen, because a real call looks like one.

## Typography

**Display, body and UI font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next Variable (OFL-1.1, Fontsource `@fontsource-variable/atkinson-hyperlegible-next` 5.3.0). Weights 400, 500, 600, plus 650 on the landing page only.
**Mono font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono Variable (OFL-1.1). Used for everything the player must read precisely or compare: the call timer, phone numbers, "the number on my card", seconds on the timeline, and codes in the replay.

**Character:** sober, clear and unambiguous. The pairing was designed for readers with low vision: I, l and 1, and O and 0, never collide. That is exactly the job of a scam drill, where a phone number or a "6-digit code" must be read correctly. The face is friendly without being cute.

**Scale: "Senior Comfortable".** This deviates from bible §3.2 Comfortable: every step is raised so that the body is 20px. The reason is our audience (SPEC §4.4).

**Settings.**

- `font-synthesis: none`.
- `font-optical-sizing: auto`.
- `text-wrap: balance` on headings; `text-wrap: pretty` on body text.
- Use the `…` character and curly quotes. Join units with `&nbsp;` ("4&nbsp;s").
- Mono numbers are grouped for reading: phone numbers as `(614) 555-0148`; codes as 3+3 (`482 913`).

**Text size setting.** "Standard" or "Larger". Larger sets the root to 120%. All tokens are in `rem`, so the whole scale follows, and so does the OS font scale.

**Loading.**

- Self-host via Fontsource. Preload the one critical Next Variable woff2 (latin subset).
- `font-display: swap` with a metric-matched local fallback, `@font-face { font-family: 'Atkinson Fallback'; src: local('Arial'); size-adjust / ascent-override measured in Stage 2 }`, so that CLS stays 0.

### Named Rules
**The Twenty Floor Rule.** Reading text is never below 20px. The 18px `meta` size is allowed only for labels of four words or fewer. Nothing is ever below 18px, including captions, footers and legal text.

**The Read-It-Exactly Rule.** Anything a player might copy, dial or compare (numbers, codes, seconds) is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono. Mono is never used for decoration or eyebrows.

**No Eyebrows Rule.** No all-caps labels and no tracked-out kickers. Hierarchy comes from size and weight, and the copy says what the thing is.

## Elevation & Depth

The **notebook** is flat and hairline-first. Cards are `surface` with a 1px `border` ring (`e1`). Shadows appear only on floating layers (sheets, toasts, the install hint) and are layered and moss-tinted (H_n 110).

## Shapes

- **Personality: Soft** (bible §5.2): xs 6 · sm 10 · md 14 · lg 20 · full.
- **Roles:**
  - stamps, keycaps and the timeline track: `xs`
  - streak days and nested tiles inside cards: `sm`
  - buttons, inputs, segmented controls, reply chips and the call-back rect: `md`
  - cards, sheets and the shared result card: `lg`
  - **true circles** (`full` on a square) for the Answer and Hang Up hardware, the timeline "you" marker and the coach's initial avatar
- **Concentric rule.** A nested element's radius = parent radius − inset when the inset is ≤ 8px. With a larger inset (cards, padding 20px), nested elements use `sm`.
- **CTA grammar: soft rectangle.** One grammar. There are no pills anywhere in the app or on the landing page. Circles are hardware, not buttons-with-text.
- **Shard geometry.** The signature moment uses 14 seeded polygons (a jittered 3 × 5 grid, with the Hang Up button's cell merged). Polygons are computed once per device from the profile id, so each person's "break" is consistent.
- **Motif.** One bespoke monoline motif: a coiled handset cord drawn as a single 1.75px stroke in `text-subtle`, ≤ 96px. It is used in empty states and the landing page's contour field. There is no other illustration.

### Signature component: The Cut (bible M15 + M5, bespoke)

**Event:** a scam call ends by the player's Hang Up or Call back with outcome `sharp` or `verified`. That happens at most once a day for scored calls; practice calls get the same moment.

- **Lesser variants:** `late` and `jumpy` get the shatter without confetti. `stayed`, `missed` and `caught` get no shatter, only a 150ms crossfade from the Line to the result.

**Build:**

- At press, the call screen's visual content is frozen (timer stopped) and cloned into 14 absolutely positioned shard copies, each clipped with `clip-path: polygon(...)` from the seeded geometry. The original is hidden.
- The result screen is already mounted underneath.
- Only `transform` and `opacity` are animated (WAAPI).
- `canvas-confetti` is lazy-loaded on the Result route and fires from the Hang Up button's centre. It uses ≤ 60 particles and the colours `accent`, `accent-dark`, `accent-soft`, `surface-2`, `border-strong` and `call-text`. **No red.**

**Performance:** shard clones have `will-change: transform` only during the 800ms window. Target 60fps on a mid-tier Android phone; QA traces it.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep reading text ≥ 20px and targets ≥ 56px. Measure them in e2e.
- **Do** keep the Line dark in both themes with AAA contrast (`call-text` 17:1, `call-muted` 11.3:1).
- **Do** set every phone number, second and code in Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, grouped for reading.
- **Do** use one filled moss button per view, placed in the bottom third on phone.
- **Do** pair every outcome with a word and an icon (✓ right call, ⓘ real call missed, ⏱ late). Colour is never the only signal.
- **Do** write callers as fictional organisations with `(NXX) 555-01xx` numbers.
- **Do** keep captions on by default and make the demo readable muted.
- **Do** let any tap finish the signature moment immediately.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use a pill. Actions are 14px soft rectangles; call hardware is a true circle. *(We never #1)*
- **Don't** use red for anything except the Hang Up circle and the scammer's ASK marker. Not even the recording indicator. *(We never #2)*
- **Don't** set reading text below 20px or any text below 18px, and don't ship a target below 56px. *(We never #3)*
- **Don't** add mascots, badges, trophies, XP, leaderboards or exclamation-mark praise. The only celebration is a family member's voice.
- **Don't** show red-flag hints during a call. The replay teaches; the call tests.
- **Don't** use shadows on the Line, or on resting notebook cards.
- **Don't** animate daily navigation. Tab switches are instant; only state changes get ≤ 200ms fades.
- **Don't** use any real bank, agency, company or person name, logo or number, anywhere: UI, scripts, tests, landing page or README.
- **Don't** use a purple or blue gradient, glassmorphism, emoji as icons, tracked-out eyebrows, or "→" on buttons (bible §2).

## Appendix B: Landing page immersive plan (bible §11)

**Decision: CSS/SVG + pre-rendered DOM capture. No live WebGL.** This is a reasoned choice under 3D_INSPIRATION §5.2's decision rule. Live WebGL requires a T-pattern that says something the flat version cannot (3P2).

The sentence this page must say is *"a real-feeling call you can cut off, and some calls are real."* The most truthful object for that sentence is **the product's own call screen** (bible P5 "show the real thing", Linear's 0-canvas precedent). A 3D handset or glyph would be a metaphor for something we can show literally.

Our audience (adult children on phones in a LinkedIn feed, and their parents on older devices) also favours a tier-0-first page.
