# Sources

Every scenario template cites at least one entry here (AC-F3-3). `scenarios:check` parses the bold
`[KEY]` markers below and fails any template that cites a key not listed. Page numbers are the printed
page numbers, read from the PDFs on 2026-10-20 during Stage 1.

- **[FTC-OA-2025]** Federal Trade Commission, *Protecting Older Consumers 2024-2025: A Report of the
  Federal Trade Commission* (report to Congress, December 1, 2025).
  https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/P144400-OlderAdultsReportDec2025.pdf
  - p.18: older consumers were 5x more likely to lose money to tech-support scams.
  - p.19: phone calls have the highest median loss.
  - p.22, fn 69: no-loss phone reports are most often about callers impersonating government and
    well-known businesses.
  - p.23: older adults were 5x more likely to report a loss on tech support, 2.7x on
    prize/sweepstakes, 1.39x on romance and 1.36x on government impersonation.
  - p.24: the highest business-impersonation losses are bank impersonators.
  - p.26: by phone, business impersonation 40%, government impersonation 23%, prizes 15% of losses.
  - p.27: gift cards are the most frequent payment method on government-impersonation,
    tech-support, romance and family/friend-impersonation scams.
- **[IC3-2025]** FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, *2025 Internet Crime Report*.
  https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf
  - p.21: call-center fraud (tech/customer support plus government impersonation).
  - p.40: grandparent and distress scams.
  - pp.44-47: victims 60+ by crime type.
- **[FTC-IMP-2026]** FTC Consumer Alert, "New trends in reports of imposter scams" (May 7, 2026).
  https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/05/new-trends-reports-imposter-scams

## How legitimate templates cite

A legitimate template is a decoy for a scam category. It cites the report passage on the scam it is the
decoy for (or on the category it belongs to), because that passage is the reason the decoy exists. A
citation on a legitimate template does not claim the call itself is in the report.
